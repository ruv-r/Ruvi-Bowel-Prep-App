# BowelPreppr
## What is this program?
BowelPreppr is a medical scheduling tool for people with an upcoming colonoscopy. Many people are in the dark about the significance of colonoscopies and the importance of bowel preparation. BowelPreppr aims to help by clearing things up and letting users know from 7 days out exactly what to do on which day. BowelPrepper also features an AI chatbot named *Prep Bud* who will help users with any questions they have.
## User story
"As a colonoscopy patient, I want a digital tool that keeps my bowel prep on track and answers my questions about the preparation."
